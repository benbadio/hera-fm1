Hardware Panel Controls

  • Left Encoders: MASTER (volume), SELECT (step scrubber), TEMPO (BPM), TRACK (T1–T4).
  • Right Encoders: K1 Cutoff, K2 Resonance, K3 Decay, K4 Accent.
  • Left Buttons: OCT- / OCT+ scroll the active octave window across the Reason grid.
  • 12 Function Buttons: FX (Randomize), SEL (Page 1/2), ENV (Keys/Curve), LFO (Engine: 303/SUB/FM/MUTE), EDIT (Shift), GLO (Settings), HOME (Track), SAVE (Flash), ARP (Bank A–D), SEQ (Pattern 1–8), PLAY/STOP, REC.

27 Silicone Keys

  • Lower 16 Keys: Step Jump / White keys (audition notes, step record in REC mode).
  • Upper 11 Keys: Black accidental keys (audition chromatic sharps/flats).
  • Step Record: Press REC, then press keys to input pitch and automatically step forward!

Multi-Engine Synthesizer

Features a real-time fixed-point 303 Acid Bassline with diode-ladder filter saturation, portamento slide across tied notes, and accent boost. Cycle engines with LFO (or Sound) for Subtractive analog leads, 2-OP FM plucks, or MUTE for silent external MIDI sequencing.
