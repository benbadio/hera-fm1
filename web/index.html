Quick Actions:

Interactive Browser Editing

  • Click the Screen Grid: Click any step and pitch row on the 240x240 LCD screen to toggle notes on/off and audition them instantly!
  • Drag Velocity Bars: Click or drag inside the lower velocity bar area to dynamically adjust gate level (0–127).
  • Draw Curves: In CURVE mode (press ENV), click inside the grid to draw CC modulation curves.
  • Step Record: Press REC, then press keys to input notes and auto-advance.

Hardware Panel Controls

  • Left Knobs: MASTER (volume), SELECT (step scrubber), TEMPO (BPM), TRACK (T1–T4).
  • Right Knobs: K1 Cutoff, K2 Resonance, K3 Decay, K4 Accent.
  • Left Buttons: OCT- / OCT+ scroll the active octave window across the grid.
  • 12 Function Buttons: FX (Random), SEL (Page 1/2), ENV (Keys/Curve), LFO (Engine: 303/SUB/FM/MUTE), EDIT (Shift), GLO (Settings), HOME (Track), SAVE (Flash), ARP (Bank A–D), SEQ (Pattern 1–8), PLAY/STOP, REC.

Multi-Engine Synthesizer

Features a real-time fixed-point 303 Acid Bassline with diode-ladder filter saturation, portamento slide across tied notes, and accent boost. Cycle engines with LFO (or Sound) for Subtractive analog leads, 2-OP FM plucks, or MUTE for silent external MIDI sequencing.
