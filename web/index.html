Sounds

64 Juno-style sounds in 8 banks. Click one to load it into the current pattern; the sliders are the eight panel knobs (K1–K4, and with GLO).

Chord sets

All 100 J-6 chord sets. Pick a set, then click a chord to hear it (chord mode turns on).

CHORD off

Phrases

9 styles × 12 variations. Styles 6–9 show the phrase itself, as measured from a J-6 (orange = accent).

PHRASE off

Sequencer

The current pattern's 64 steps. Click a step to edit it on the device (then play keys to enter notes, SEQ to finish).
